# NBA v0.9 research — 2026-10-28

Research only. Winner-strategy paper trading continues separately. No prop or combo entries, RFQs, authenticated orders, or live execution are enabled by this document. Availability and rules vary by event and change over time; always archive and validate each contract's actual metadata and rules.

## Market inventory and settlement

| Family | Verified examples and rule considerations |
| --- | --- |
| Player stats | The [PRA event](https://kalshi.com/markets/kxnbapra/nba-player-points--rebounds--assists/kxnbapra-26oct27bknbos) provides combined points, rebounds and assists thresholds. Its rules say inactive or never-entered players resolve at fair price; playing even briefly invokes actual stats. A game starting within 48 hours after postponement remains eligible; otherwise it resolves at fair price. Other individual stats listed on [Kalshi's NBA event page](https://kalshi.com/markets/kxnba2qspread/nba-2nd-quarter-spread/kxnba2qspread-26oct20okcsas) include points, rebounds, assists, threes, steals and blocks. Verify each actual rule and line rather than assume PRA rules apply. |
| Quarter | [1Q total](https://kalshi.com/markets/kxnba1qtotal/nba-1st-quarter-total/kxnba1qtotal-26oct27detnyk) counts only first-quarter points. [2Q spread](https://kalshi.com/markets/kxnba2qspread/nba-2nd-quarter-spread/kxnba2qspread-26oct20okcsas) is separately listed. The example rules state 4Q excludes overtime. |
| Half | [1H total](https://kalshi.com/markets/kxnba1htotal/nba-1st-half-total/kxnba1htotal-26oct27detnyk) measures only first-half points; its rules note 2H includes overtime. |
| Team total | [Cleveland team total](https://kalshi.com/markets/kxnbateamtotal/nba-team-total/kxnbateamtotal-26oct28wascle) defines team, matchup, date, threshold and postponement/fair-price treatment; ESPN and governing league are verification sources. |
| Combos | [Kalshi's combo documentation](https://help.kalshi.com/en/articles/13823820-combos) describes distinct combo markets/order books and resolution as the product of underlying positions. Eligible components change over time; its [basketball incentive list](https://help.kalshi.com/en/articles/17184676-sports-prop-combo-market-component-legs-liquidity-incentive-program) includes player categories, quarter/half winners, spreads, totals and team totals. Program eligibility alone does not establish that any particular leg combination is currently quotable. |

Example observations are not an exhaustive live market census; quoted screenshots/pages do not prove executable depth. Archive raw event, market, rule version, timestamps, and both sides of the order book. Parse player ID, normalized team, date, game and period, threshold strictness and side, OT, cancellation and fair-price terms. Reject missing/ambiguous player, date, line, rules, duplicate events and stale quotes. Do not derive a player's game identity from a name alone.

## RFQ and combination research

[Create RFQ](https://docs.kalshi.com/api-reference/communications/create-rfq) and [Create Quote](https://docs.kalshi.com/api-reference/communications/create-quote) are authenticated communications endpoints; a request, quote, acceptance and confirmation are distinct steps, subject to available collateral, execution and platform rules. [Kalshi's combo guide](https://help.kalshi.com/en/articles/13823820-combos) says quotes may change and fills are not guaranteed. [Multivariate events](https://docs.kalshi.com/api-reference/events/get-multivariate-events) expose selected legs and settlement values; the [old multivariate lookup](https://docs.kalshi.com/api-reference/multivariate/lookup-tickers-for-market-in-multivariate-event-collection) is deprecated. Do not submit RFQs or quotes for research: they are not passive public snapshots and can lead to execution. Public combo-market observations, if available, are not proof that an arbitrary desired combination could have been filled.

## Provider evaluation

* [BALLDONTLIE NBA API](https://docs.balldontlie.io/) documents game/player stats (including realized minutes), injuries, box scores, lineups, and period-filtered advanced stats. Play-by-play starts in the 2025 NBA season. Historical minutes are not a pregame minutes forecast. Account tier, coverage, refresh timing, injury history and permissions for the existing paid subscription remain unverified; verify with account owner and read-only calls. This is not a claim that historical point-in-time injury/lineup snapshots exist.
* [BALLDONTLIE Lab](https://lab.balldontlie.io/docs/api/) is a separate product with NBA player-prop models for points, rebounds, assists, threes and other stats; its API requires Lab PRO or ALL-ACCESS and documented NBA prop history begins in 2024–25. It is unknown whether current subscription includes it. Its prop backtests exclude one-sided milestone markets, so they cannot directly validate Kalshi milestone contracts.
* [SportsDataIO](https://sportsdata.io/developers/coverages/nba) documents game-level player projections, injuries, and projected/confirmed lineups; [its API](https://sportsdata.io/developers/api-documentation/nba) describes live and final play-by-play. A demonstrable gap would be missing point-in-time lineup/injury history or pregame minutes/stat forecasts with sufficient refresh and licensing; first verify access, history, timeliness, fields, costs, licensing and backfill on a sample.
* [Sportradar NBA play-by-play](https://developer.sportradar.com/basketball/v5/reference/nba-play-by-play) documents detailed real-time game events; [Synergy player projections](https://developer.sportradar.com/basketball/reference/get_advanced-basketball-league-playerprojections) documents forward-looking per-game projections including NBA. Verify product entitlement, historical as-of snapshots, latency, licenses, cost and fields; these pages do not prove they are included in one package. Better historical play-by-play, rotation/lineup history or reliable granular updates may justify purchase only if proven to improve out-of-sample utility.

No provider purchase is authorized. Run an evidence-based comparison on the same as-of games, missingness, freshness, historical depth, correction policy and operational cost before requesting approval.

## Research architecture and models

1. Isolate provider adapters and normalize stable league/team/player/game IDs. Persist raw responses plus source, observed/available times, retrieval errors, corrections, hashes and schema versions in append-only snapshots. Never use post-tip updates as pregame features; missingness invalidates dependent forecasts.
2. Match Kalshi game, team, player, category, threshold, time period and settlement rules using schedule plus independently verified roster identity. Preserve raw text and rule hash. Unknown or ambiguous identity/settlement => reject. Treat fair-price and nonparticipation as distinct outcomes rather than a binary loss.
3. Estimate a participation probability and conditional minutes distribution from strictly pregame information; model role, injury status, rest and lineup uncertainty. Use separate and calibrated conditional distributions: points via attempts and scoring mix/efficiency; rebounds via rebound chances and capture rate; assists via teammate baskets and assist share; threes via attempts and make rate. Hierarchical shrinkage and overdispersion account for player sparsity; simulate full distributions, not just averages. Validate discrete thresholds and overtime treatment.
4. For quarter totals, predict possessions and points per possession jointly by team, quarter, rotation, rest and historical pace. Model 1Q–4Q separately; derive halves from joint quarter draws and model overtime separately where rules require it. Validate reconstructed period scoring against official box scores, including corrections.
5. For same-game legs, simulate shared pace, lineups, minutes, game state and shooting/usage factors, then evaluate each contract's rule against the same simulated game. Fit and validate cross-player and cross-stat dependence on strictly prior data; compare against independent-leg and empirical baselines, report uncertainty and stress scenarios. Do not multiply marginals as the production estimate. Reject contradictory legs and treat pushes, fair-price outcomes and partial settlement explicitly.
6. Store immutable forecasts, model/version/data hashes, pregame evidence, matching and explicit rejection reasons. Evaluate per family with rolling time splits, log loss, Brier/reliability by threshold and subgroup, precision of event/settlement mapping, coverage and uncertainty. Tune only on training/validation, use untouched temporal holdouts, and compare against contemporaneous executable market prices after fees and slippage. Treat corrected outcomes, lineup leakage and survivor selection as explicit checks.
7. Only after matching, out-of-sample calibration, simulation, risk and settlement tests pass, consider an isolated, disabled-by-default paper adapter. Require valid fresh quotes with depth, verified fees, conservative fills and correlated portfolio caps. Public prices cannot demonstrate RFQ execution. Real trading stays disabled and winner strategy limits remain unchanged.
